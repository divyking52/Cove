07 — Planning process

Four steps from brief to live.

01

Discover

We understand your objectives, audience, and the kind of gathering this needs to be.

  • Purpose, audience, and the tone the room should hold
  • Dates, cities, and the constraints we design around
  • What success looks like when everyone is in the same place
02

Design

We build the ideal experience — destination, rhythm, rooms, and the hours in between.

  • Destination, venue, and the shape of the days
  • Rooms, stages, and the quieter hours around them
  • Travel architecture that keeps the programme intact
03

Coordinate

We manage travel and event logistics so the programme holds together on the ground.

  • Flights, hotels, and transfers as one sequence
  • Suppliers, run-of-show, and on-site timing
  • A single point of contact while the pieces move
04

Experience

We make everything happen seamlessly, with a team present for the live moment.

  • On-the-ground hosting from arrival to close
  • Live adjustments so the programme still feels effortless
  • Presence for the moment the gathering was built for
